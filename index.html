Apache Wicket site designs

Designs for wicket.apache.org, published side by side for comparison. The sketches show a homepage only; the full designs include every page. Content on the older designs is as of their date.

  1. The departure board

    October 2026

    Station hall with a departure board of release lines, built for Wicket 11.0 and the quarterly release model.

  2. The reveal

    October 2026

    The reveal as a complete site: monochrome with Wicket orange, a homepage that builds a page's component tree step by step, and every page restyled.

  3. Sketch: grid system

    October 2026

    Homepage sketch in the Swiss International Typographic Style: a module grid with one committed field colour.

  4. Sketch: drawing sheet

    October 2026

    Homepage sketch as an engineering drawing: Wicket drawn as a part in two views, releases in the revision table.

  5. Sketch: reveal

    October 2026

    Monochrome with Wicket orange, after vercel.com/eve: scroll through seven steps that build a page's component tree in real code.

  6. Sketch: component tree on bookcloth

    October 2026

    The original provenance ribbon's world without a photo: the component-tree ribbon enters from the left edge and opens the page.

  7. Sketch: ribbon as component tree

    October 2026

    The ribbon runs through a page's component tree, joining each Java component to its tag; release facts shrink to one small block.

  8. Sketch: ribbon with seasons

    October 2026

    The provenance ribbon under a seasonal photograph, with the big logo lockup and a light body. Try ?season=summer or ?occasion=halloween.

  9. Sketch: provenance ribbon

    October 2026

    Homepage sketch that follows Wicket's release lines as one ribbon through twenty years, splitting at the present.
